Family Tiger Pass

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 August 2026

Family Tiger Pass (“the app”) is a local iPhone utility. It estimates food portions, crate class, treat budgets, walk windows, and travel kits for dogs and cats in one household. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

What we collect

The app does not create an account, does not sign you in, and does not send household data to our servers. We do not operate a backend for this product.

Figures you type (weights, bag size, hours away, and similar inputs) and the projects you save stay in the app’s local store on that iPhone (SwiftData). A small onboarding flag is stored in UserDefaults so the intro pages do not repeat.

The app does not access the camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, or location. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. App Tracking Transparency is not used. We do not sell personal information.

What leaves the phone

Opening Privacy or Terms in Settings loads this website in Safari. That request goes to the host serving these pages (Cloudflare Pages). We do not use that visit to identify you in the app.

If you export a PDF, the file is created on the device. Sharing it uses the iOS share sheet you choose (Mail, Files, and so on). We do not receive a copy.

Children

The app is a household kitchen tool, not directed at children under 13. Do not enter someone else’s personal data that you do not have a right to keep on the phone.

Deleting data

Settings → Delete All Data removes saved projects and results and returns the intro screens. Uninstalling the app also removes the local store.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you have read the new text.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support link on the Family Tiger Pass App Store product page.
